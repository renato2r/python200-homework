BrightLeaf Solar Employee Benefits Policy
Available to all full-time employees after 30 days of continuous employment.

Health & Wellness:
- Comprehensive medical insurance provided by Blue Shield, with 80% of premiums covered by BrightLeaf.
- Fully covered dental and vision insurance for the employee, with optional family add-ons.

Retirement Plan:
- BrightLeaf offers a 401(k) retirement savings plan through Vanguard.
- The company provides a dollar-for-dollar matching contribution up to 4% of the employee's annual salary. Fully vested after 1 year.

Paid Time Off (PTO):
- Full-time employees accrue 15 days of paid vacation per year, calculated monthly.
- 5 days of paid sick leave are provided upfront at the start of each calendar year.
