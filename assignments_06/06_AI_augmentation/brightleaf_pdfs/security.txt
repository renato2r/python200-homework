BrightLeaf Solar Corporate Security and Information Protection Policy

Data Protection:
- All company data must be treated as confidential. Customer financial details and solar design schematics must never be shared externally without legal approval.

Password & Authentication Rules:
- Passwords for all internal systems must be a minimum of 12 characters, including uppercase letters, numbers, and special characters.
- Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) is strictly mandatory for accessing Google Workspace, AWS, and company CRM tools.

Device Compliance:
- Work laptops must have active firewall protection and automated software updates enabled.
- Screens must lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity to prevent physical building unauthorized access.
